PRIVACY POLICY

This portfolio presents Eng-Angluos Rezq's work. Browsing does not require a visitor account or a contact form submission.

The site stores your Arabic/English choice in your browser. The private admin area uses browser storage for its sign-in session. You can remove stored data through your browser settings.

Hosting and content providers receive technical requests when pages, images and fonts load. The site uses Cloudflare hosting, Google Fonts and some externally hosted project screenshots. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Contact links open WhatsApp, email or other external platforms. If you contact me, the information you choose to send is processed through that platform and used to respond to your inquiry. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents through the portfolio.

For privacy questions: angluosr@gmail.com.
